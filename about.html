Who We Are

We Help You Explore GB

GBExplorer is a travel platform created to make discovering Gilgit Baltistan easier, more enjoyable and more memorable.

Our goal is to help travelers find beautiful destinations, understand the region, plan their journeys and connect with unforgettable experiences.

Whether you are visiting Hunza for the first time, planning an adventure in Skardu or dreaming about the Karakoram Highway, GBExplorer is here to help.
